Who I am.

Thank you for reading this far.

For a long time I thought I had to choose between calculation and drawing. I studied engineering and, in the meantime, taught myself design, project by project, since 2018. Over the years I understood they were not two separate paths: engineering taught me responsibility and perseverance, to measure before giving an opinion and to document properly; design taught me to notice how a place feels and to listen to what someone needs before drawing it. Today I work with both at once.

The last few years have been about travelling and working. I worked independently and in studios with large teams within my profession; I was also a kitchen hand, made beds in hotels, washed dishes in restaurants and served large events in Italy and small ones in Australia. That taught me the value of being on time, doing well whatever needs doing and looking after the person in front of you. It also taught me to work side by side with people from everywhere, to keep calm when the pace picks up and, along the way, to cook much better.

I like to ask a lot of questions before starting and to explain the reason behind every decision.

I have been travelling since I finished my degree and my curiosity hasn't worn off: almost twenty countries so far, some only passing through. That's why I carry a second-hand camera everywhere: to capture what I don't want to forget.

If you're reading this, you're probably looking for someone for your studio, your restaurant, your shop or your hotel. I'd love to tell you more in person. Write to me.
